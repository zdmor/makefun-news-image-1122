Climate · Antalya, Türkiye

COP31 collapses: no climate deal

After a record 48 hours of overtime, the UN climate summit ended with no agreement at all. The EU and island states rejected a final text that never mentioned fossil fuels — the chief cause of global warming.

United States

Democrats’ biggest House win since 1992

A recount in North Carolina gave Democrat Chuck Hubbard a 330-vote win over 11-term Republican Virginia Foxx — the party’s 258th House seat.

The Gulf

Two supertankers hit mines off Hormuz

Iran’s navy said two supertankers struck naval mines and caught fire east of the Strait of Hormuz, as its naval blockade and US escorts continue.

Sudan

Drones kill four near Khartoum, hit UN building

Drone attacks blamed on the Rapid Support Forces killed four people around Sudan’s capital and damaged a United Nations building while staff were inside.

Libya

Russian warship sunk by sea drones

A suspected drone strike sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi port; satellite images showed it capsized, only its bow above water.

Indonesia: factory fire kills 3, dozens missing

A blaze at a candy factory in Cikande, western Java, killed at least three workers; rescuers searched the ruins for dozens still missing.

Demonstrations over prices and housing entered a third straight day across Europe, from Portugal to France.
